All Information About MBA at SPJIMR Mumbai

SPJIMR Mumbai - MBA Program

Specializations Offered:

  • Finance
  • Marketing
  • Operations & Supply Chain
  • Information Management
  • Human Resource Management

Eligibility Criteria:

Bachelor’s degree with minimum 50% marks. Valid CAT/XAT/GMAT score. Minimum 2 years of work experience preferred.

Fee Structure:

Program Fee: ₹24,00,000 (approx. for entire course)

Includes tuition, hostel, and other academic charges.

Admission Process:

Apply online via SPJIMR website. Shortlisting based on profile and entrance exam. Followed by WAT and PI rounds.

Scholarship:

  • Need-based financial assistance
  • Merit-based scholarships

Reservation Policy:

No government-based reservations. SPJIMR follows profile-based selection with diversity considerations.
