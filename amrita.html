Amrita University - MCA Program

MCA Specializations Offered:

  • Data Science
  • Cyber Security
  • Artificial Intelligence

Eligibility Criteria:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Applications or related field with at least 60% marks. Admission through entrance test conducted by the university or through CUET-PG.

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per year): ₹1,50,000

Hostel Fee (per year): ₹50,000

Other Charges: ₹10,000

Total (1st Year): ₹2.1 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply online via Amrita admission portal. Appear for entrance/CUET exam. Selection based on merit list.

Scholarship:

  • Merit-based scholarships
  • Financial assistance for economically weaker sections
  • Special scholarships for girl students

Reservation Policy:

As per Government of India norms. Reservation for SC/ST/OBC/EWS/PwD categories.
