All Information About MCA at VIT Vellore

VIT Vellore - MCA Program

Eligibility Criteria:

Bachelor’s degree with 60% marks in Mathematics or statistics at 10+2 or graduation level. Admission through VITMEE exam.

MCA Specializations Offered:

  • Data Science & Analytics
  • Software Engineering
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Cybersecurity

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per semester): ₹85,000

Hostel Fee (per semester): ₹35,000

One-time Admission Fee: ₹10,000

Other Charges (per semester): ₹5,000

Total (1st Year): ₹2.3–2.5 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply through the VIT official website. Appear for the VITMEE exam. Admission based on merit and counseling.

Scholarship:

  • Scholarship for top VITMEE rankers
  • Need-based scholarships for economically weaker sections
  • Special schemes for girl students and rural students

Reservation Policy:

VIT does not follow caste-based reservation. Seats are allotted purely on merit through VITMEE ranking.
