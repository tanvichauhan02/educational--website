All About MBA at ISB Hyderabad

ISB Hyderabad - MBA Program

Program Highlights:

  • Post Graduate Programme in Management (PGP)
  • 1-Year Full-Time Residential MBA
  • Global faculty and international exposure
  • World-class infrastructure

Eligibility Criteria:

A bachelor’s degree in any discipline. Valid GMAT/GRE score. Minimum 2 years of full-time work experience preferred.

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee: ₹38,00,000

Accommodation & Food: ₹2,50,000

Admission Fee: ₹3,00,000

Total Cost: ₹43–45 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply online through the ISB admissions portal. Submit test scores, work experience, essays, and recommendations. Personal interview for shortlisted candidates.

Scholarship:

  • Merit-based scholarships
  • Diversity and Need-based aid
  • Corporate & Alumni-sponsored scholarships

Reservation Policy:

ISB follows a merit-based selection. No caste-based reservations.
