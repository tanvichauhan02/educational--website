All Information About MCA at Manipal University

Manipal University - MCA Program

Eligibility Criteria:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science / IT or equivalent with minimum 50% marks. Admission via online entrance test.

MCA Specializations:

  • Software Engineering
  • Data Science
  • Cyber Security
  • AI & Machine Learning

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per semester): ₹95,000

Hostel Fee (per semester): ₹45,000

One-time Admission Fee: ₹10,000

Other Charges (per semester): ₹5,000

Total (1st Year): ₹3.1–3.3 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply online via the university portal. Appear for the online entrance exam. Selection based on merit & entrance score.

Scholarship:

  • Scholarships based on entrance rank
  • Special discounts for girl students
  • Financial assistance for low-income families

Reservation Policy:

Reservation for SC/ST/OBC as per government norms. Separate seats for Karnataka domicile students.
