All Information About MBA at XLRI Jamshedpur

XLRI Jamshedpur - MBA Program

MBA Programs Offered:

  • PGDM (Human Resource Management)
  • PGDM (Business Management)
  • Executive PGDM

Eligibility Criteria:

Bachelor's degree in any discipline with minimum 50% marks. Candidates must appear in XAT exam conducted by XLRI or GMAT for international candidates.

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (2 years): ₹23,60,000

Hostel and Other Charges: ₹3,00,000 approx.

Total Program Fee: ₹26.5–27 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Register for XAT or apply through GMAT. Shortlisted candidates are invited for interview rounds. Final selection based on test score, academics, work experience, and interview performance.

Scholarship:

  • Merit-based scholarships for top performers
  • Need-based financial aid for deserving students
  • Scholarships supported by alumni and corporates

Reservation Policy:

No government-based reservations. XLRI promotes diversity and inclusion through holistic selection.
