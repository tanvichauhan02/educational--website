All Information About BCA at MIT Pune

MIT Pune - BCA Program

Specializations/Focus Areas:

  • Data Analytics
  • Web Development
  • Mobile App Development
  • Cloud Computing
  • Cyber Security

Eligibility Criteria:

Passed 10+2 or equivalent from any stream with minimum 50% marks. Preference given to students with Math/CS. Admission through merit or entrance exam.

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per year): ₹1,10,000

Registration Fee (one-time): ₹10,000

Hostel Fee (per year): ₹85,000

Other Charges: ₹5,000

Total (1st Year): ₹2.1–2.2 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply online on MIT WPU's official site. Shortlisting based on merit or entrance. Document verification and fee payment confirm admission.

Scholarship:

  • Scholarships for top scorers in 12th
  • Special financial aid for economically weaker sections
  • Merit-based continuation scholarships

Reservation Policy:

As per Government of Maharashtra norms for reserved categories and minority institutions.
