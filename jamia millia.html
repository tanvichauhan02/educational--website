All Information About BCA at Jamia Millia Islamia

Jamia Millia Islamia - BCA Program

BCA Program Overview:

  • Full-time 3-year undergraduate course
  • Focus on Computer Applications & Programming
  • Modern labs and tech infrastructure

Eligibility Criteria:

10+2 or equivalent with Mathematics. Minimum 50% marks. Admission via entrance test conducted by Jamia.

Fee Structure:

Annual Fee: ₹8,500 approx.

Hostel Fee: ₹6,500 approx. (if availed)

Exam & Other Charges: ₹2,000 approx.

Total (1st Year): ₹17,000 approx.

Admission Process:

Apply through JMI portal. Appear in entrance test. Merit-based selection as per test scores.

Scholarship:

  • Central & State Scholarships
  • Merit-cum-means scholarships
  • Financial aid for minorities & EWS

Reservation Policy:

Reservations as per government norms: SC/ST/OBC/Minorities/Disabled categories.
