Complete Details About BBA at NMIMS Mumbai

NMIMS Mumbai - BBA Program

BBA Specializations Offered:

  • Marketing Management
  • Finance & Accounts
  • Human Resource Management
  • International Business
  • Operations & Supply Chain

Eligibility Criteria:

Minimum 60% aggregate in 10+2 or equivalent. Admission through NPAT exam. English as a compulsory subject.

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per year): ₹3,50,000 approx.

Hostel Fee (per year): ₹1,50,000 approx.

One-time Registration Fee: ₹10,000

Total (1st Year): ₹5–5.2 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply through NMIMS portal. Appear for NPAT exam. Shortlisting based on merit & score in NPAT.

Scholarship:

  • Merit-based scholarships for high NPAT scorers
  • Financial aid based on need & income

Reservation Policy:

No category-based reservations. All admissions based on merit and entrance exam performance.
