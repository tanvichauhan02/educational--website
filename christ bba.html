All Information About BBA at Christ University

Christ University - BBA Program

BBA Specializations Offered:

  • Finance & Accounting
  • Marketing Management
  • Human Resource Management
  • International Business
  • Business Analytics

Eligibility Criteria:

Pass in 10+2 (any stream) with minimum 60% marks. Selection through Christ University Entrance Test followed by Micro Presentation and Interview.

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per year): ₹1,80,000 – ₹2,50,000 (varies by specialization)

Hostel Fee (optional, per year): ₹70,000 – ₹1,00,000

Other Charges (Exam, Admin etc.): ₹10,000 approx.

Total (1st Year): ₹2.5–3.5 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply online through the Christ University portal. Appear for CUET + MP + Interview. Final selection based on overall performance.

Scholarship:

  • Merit scholarships for high scorers
  • Financial aid for economically weaker sections
  • Special scholarships for sports/cultural achievers

Reservation Policy:

No caste/category-based reservation. Selection purely on merit and performance.
