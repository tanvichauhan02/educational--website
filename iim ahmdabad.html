All Information About MBA at IIM Ahmedabad

IIM Ahmedabad - MBA Program

Specializations Offered:

  • Finance
  • Marketing
  • Operations
  • Human Resource Management
  • Information Systems

Eligibility Criteria:

Bachelor's degree with at least 50% marks. Final-year students can also apply. Admission via CAT followed by WAT & PI.

Fee Structure:

Total Program Fee (2 years): ₹25,00,000 approx.

Includes tuition, hostel, and other academic charges.

Admission Process:

Register for CAT. Shortlisting based on CAT percentile. Final selection includes Written Ability Test (WAT) and Personal Interview (PI).

Scholarship:

  • Merit-cum-Means Scholarships
  • Scholarships from Alumni Associations
  • Financial aid for economically weaker students

Reservation Policy:

As per Government of India norms for SC, ST, OBC-NCL, EWS, and PwD categories.
