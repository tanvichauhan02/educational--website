All Information About BBA at Symbiosis Pune

Symbiosis International University, Pune – BBA Program

BBA Specializations Offered:

  • Marketing Management
  • Human Resource Management
  • International Business
  • Accounting & Finance
  • Environment Management

Eligibility Criteria:

Passed 10+2 from a recognized board with minimum 50% marks (45% for SC/ST). Admission through SET (Symbiosis Entrance Test).

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per year): ₹3,40,000

Hostel Fee (per year): ₹1,20,000 approx.

One-time Admission Fee: ₹20,000

Miscellaneous Charges: ₹10,000

Total (1st Year): ₹4.9–5.1 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply online through SET portal. Appear for SET exam. Based on SET score and personal interview, final selection is made.

Scholarship:

  • Merit scholarships based on SET performance
  • Financial aid for economically weaker students
  • Sports and special category scholarships

Reservation Policy:

As per Government of India norms: SC (15%), ST (7.5%), Differently Abled (3%), and others.
