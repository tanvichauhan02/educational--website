All Information About BBA at Amity Noida

Amity University Noida - BBA Program

BBA Specializations Offered:

  • BBA General
  • BBA International Business
  • BBA Finance & Accounting
  • BBA Marketing & Sales
  • BBA Family Business & Entrepreneurship

Eligibility Criteria:

Minimum 60% marks in 10+2. Admission is based on merit and personal interview.

Fee Structure:

Tuition Fee (per semester): ₹1,85,000

Hostel Fee (per semester): ₹40,000 – ₹60,000

One-time Admission Fee: ₹20,000

Total (1st Year): ₹4.5–5.2 lakh approx.

Admission Process:

Apply online via Amity admission portal. Shortlisted candidates appear for personal interview.

Scholarship:

  • 100% Scholarship for 93%+ in 12th (CBSE/ISC)
  • 50% for 88%+, 25% for 80%+
  • Financial aid available

Reservation Policy:

No reservation. Admissions are merit-based and open to all categories.
